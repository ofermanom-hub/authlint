🔐 AuthLint

A linter for enterprise auth configs. Paste your SAML metadata, a JWT, or an OIDC discovery URL — get a 15-year-implementation-veteran's review in 5 seconds.

Nothing is stored. Inputs are processed in-memory only.

Scanning… Findings sorted critical → ok

What it checks

  • SAML: cert expiry & key size, signing/digest algorithm, SSO/SLO endpoints, NameID format, signing posture (AuthnRequestsSigned, WantAssertionsSigned), attribute coverage.
  • JWT: alg: none & HMAC misuse, missing standard claims, lifetime sanity, kid rotation hygiene.
  • OIDC: deprecated implicit/ROPC flows, PKCE support, id_token signing alg, JWKS reachability + key rotation, HTTPS posture.
